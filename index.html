Direct World Bank REST Integration

Query verified annual indicator series directly from the World Bank API without intermediary scraping. Includes automated validation, missing-year detection, and tabular calculations.

  • Inflation CPI (FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG) — Annual percentage change in consumer price indices.
  • Unemployment (SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS) — Total percentage of national labor force (ILO modeled estimate).
  • Real GDP Growth (NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG) — Annual growth rate of market prices at constant local currency.
// World Bank Query Specification
GET /v2/country/IN/indicator/FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG
Query Parameters: date=2015:2025&format=json
[HTTP/1.1 200 OK]
{
"country": "India",
"indicator": "Inflation, consumer prices (%)",
"latest_obs": <annual_value>,
"status": "audited_world_bank_record"
}

Academic Epistemic Guardrails

The interpretation engine pairs empirical observations with news coverage through deterministic Python templates. It explicitly refuses to claim causation from correlation or temporal alignment.

  • Transmission Explanations — Outlines plausible economic mechanisms without declaring causality.
  • Reporting Lag Awareness — Contrasts annual statistical updates with fast-moving weekly headlines.
  • Auditable Output — Every sentence traces directly to verified input records.

Academic Notice & Epistemic Boundaries (Non-Causal Rule)
1. Reporting Lag Discrepancy: Official macroeconomic indicators from the World Bank are collected annually with verification lags, whereas financial news coverage tracks weekly sentiment.
2. Correlation ≠ Causation: Semantic alignment between news topics and indicator trends highlights potential economic transmission mechanisms, but does not prove direct causality.
3. Auditable Transparency: All conclusions are generated using deterministic Python templates and transparent keyword lexicons. Zero generative LLMs are used to fabricate conclusions.
