04 - The analytics suite

Four honest lenses.

Deterministic, fully transparent analyses - fixed rules and published lexicons, no AI and no black boxes. The same methods run in the Streamlit app.

News Pulse

Reads the tone of every headline with the published VADER lexicon - offline and deterministic.

Shock Radar

Flags years where an indicator moved unusually sharply versus its own history (a z-score rule).

Outlook Engine

Extends each indicator's straight-line trend a few years forward. Illustrative only, never a prediction.

LinkLens

Checks whether news tone and indicator changes move together over genuinely overlapping years. Correlation is not causation.

07 - About the data

Honest by design.

Yearly, not monthly

World Bank indicators are annual. The latest year is often a preliminary estimate and gets revised. Nothing here is estimated between years.

News is a snapshot

Headlines come from a live news feed and can be older than the latest data year. Each one shows its own date. Keyword topics and TF-IDF terms can misfile a story.

Not advice

Seeing two things move together does not show one caused the other. This is a learning tool, not investment or policy advice.
